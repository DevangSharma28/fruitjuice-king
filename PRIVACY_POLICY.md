This policy explains what information **Juice King Tycoon** (the "Game"), published by **Ionix Games** ("we", "us"),
collects and how it is used.

## 1. Information we collect

**We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.** The
Game has no accounts and no chat.

### Game progress (stays on your device)
Your progress (money, unlocks, upgrades, Golden Apples, Ad Tickets, settings) is saved **only on your device**. We do
not receive it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

### Purchases
In-app purchases are processed by **Google Play**. We never see your payment details. The Game receives a purchase
confirmation and a transaction ID from Google Play, which are kept on your device so a purchase is delivered once
and your VIP purchase can be restored.

### Advertising
The Game shows optional **rewarded video ads** that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are
provided by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

- your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
- device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
- ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and diagnostic data.

How Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads or
Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads).

### Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)
If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through
Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings ▸ Privacy** in
the Game.

## 2. How information is used
- to run the Game and save your progress on your device;
- to deliver and restore purchases;
- to show rewarded ads and keep them free of fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Sharing
Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Play for purchases, Google AdMob for ads), which
process it under their own privacy policies. Data is encrypted in transit.

## 4. Children
The Game is intended for players **aged 13 and over**. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through
the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 5. Data retention and deletion
Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. We hold no account data about you, so there is
nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies; you can
reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or
delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests
are handled by the advertising provider: see the Google links above. You can also contact us.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the
Game or on its store page.
